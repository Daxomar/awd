Privacy and community rules

Accra We Dey is a game for adults (18+). This page explains what the game keeps about you, why, who can see it, and the rules everyone plays by. It describes how the game works today.

Community rules

  • No harassment, threats, hate, or sexual content involving anyone under 18.
  • No scams, and never trade game money, items or accounts for real money. Game cedis have no cash value.
  • Do not share anyone's personal details (phone numbers, addresses, photos) without their consent.
  • Event descriptions, profiles, posts and advert text must follow the same rules. Text is filtered automatically and moderators can remove it.
  • Breaking the rules can lead to a warning, a temporary suspension (you cannot sign in until it ends) or a ban. Your saved game is kept either way.

Your location in the game is never shown on public profiles. Payment card and mobile-money details are entered with the payment provider and never reach the game.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your username, character, public profile details you choose to show, and your net-worth ranking.
  • Staff use a separate, access-controlled admin tool. Each staff role sees only what it needs; for example only finance staff and owners see payments. Staff cannot read your password or change your wallet, and every staff action is recorded in a log that cannot be edited.
  • Private messages are protected by access control but are not end-to-end encrypted. Staff do not see message text in the admin tool, only counts.

Your safety tools

  • Block anyone from their player card: they can no longer message you.
  • Report a player, a post or an event. Reports go to moderators.
  • Discovery: you choose whether you can be suggested to new players, and you can opt out of introductions.
  • Romance requests can be turned off in Settings.
  • Account security in Settings lets you change your password or sign out of every device.

To appeal a suspension or ask for your account to be deleted, use the report option in the game; an owner handles the request.

Advertising content

  • Adverts must be lawful, honest and suitable for adults in a public city. Adverts about gambling, investment schemes, health cures or adult content are refused or held for a moderator; they never go live automatically.
  • You must own the rights to the artwork and name you upload. Links must use https and may not be shortened.
  • Campaigns that pass the automatic checks go live after verified payment; anything else waits for a moderator. Moderators can suspend a campaign that breaks these rules.
