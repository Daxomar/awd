How it works
  • Payment is confirmed by our server with Paystack. Returning to this page is not treated as proof of payment.
  • Automatic activation: when the artwork decodes safely, the name passes our filter, the link is on the allowlist and the placement is still reserved for you, your campaign goes live right after verified payment. Exclusive boards start when the current booking ends.
  • Automated checks cannot judge every image or claim. Campaigns they cannot clear wait for a moderator, and moderators can suspend any campaign that breaks the rules: no scams, gambling, adult content, weapons, hate or misleading claims.
  • Ground panels rent an in-game promotional surface only. They do not transfer ownership of any real or in-game land or property.
  • Impressions and clicks are estimates from measured in-game visibility, counted at most once per player every 15 minutes. Real-world reach is not guaranteed.
  • Late payments after a reservation expires, duplicate charges and refunds go to review; your reference is always shown here.
