# Stage E: workplace participation and economy proposal

Audit: all 19 careers already use authoritative one-real-hour attendance, skills, performance, weekday/time schedules and promotions. Career `mg` catalogue fields are stale: the current shift RPC refuses client task performance and never launches those games. Separate employment-only gig actions launch trotro (`mate`), kitchen (`rush`) and debugging (`freelance`) minigames. Dance/boxing are recreational as well as career themes and stay playable. Oware and phone games are unrelated and preserved.

Before applying the replacement: salary rank tables, grade multipliers, workday budgets, shift duration, promotion thresholds and saved employment remain unchanged. No task cash or dormant minigame salary bonus is introduced. Retired three gig actions retain IDs/rest/workday limits and switch to timed server-authoritative assignments, with a fixed base GH₵62 (the old two-star outcome from GH₵18 + 22×stars), skill scaling 7% per level, rather than client-reported 0–3 stars (GH₵18–84). This removes client scoring as a cash input, while keeping the middle outcome and existing fatigue multipliers. The duration becomes one existing compressed 60-game-minute task. This modest normalization requires economy review before production rollout, not a silent salary increase.

Each career receives three authored workplace assignments at existing furniture: transport dispatch, food preparation operations, retail stock/customer work, technical diagnosis, banking queues, hospital administration (not medical advice), policing records, media operations, event sound, training safety, academic preparation, hotel reception, aviation ground checks, civic administration, vehicle handover, fishing equipment, garment finishing and community administration. Work is chosen at its physical station, takes server-measured time, and completes only while still near the station. Distinct choices affect existing performance, satisfaction and skill, not cash directly. Assignments are stable per shift with no duplicate completion or client reward RPC. Task cash is zero.

The phone describes attendance/schedule and current assignment; the existing room context sheet provides station choices. No periodic modal prompts. Players may ignore optional assignments and retain ordinary attendance-based wages; task quality helps existing progression. Shift state is saved, but existing reconnect policy still cancels active shifts while away. Completed career records/skills/balances persist. This milestone does not add a cross-city delivery routing career or change architecture.
