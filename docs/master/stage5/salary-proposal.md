## Normalised unit

One paid **shift** = one fictional 8-hour shift = one active work session (three
assignments, about 3–4 real minutes) or one auto-shift (70% pay), at most once per
real calendar day on the career's work days. Weekly income = shift pay × work days.

## Cost of living used (cheapest life)

Rent (Nima single room): GH₵110/week · food (three ~GH₵15 meals a day): GH₵315/week ·
trotro commute (6 km each way): about GH₵144/week for six work days.
**Basic living ≈ GH₵425/week before transport.** Hustler start: GH₵800; Dbee start: GH₵25,000.

## Findings

- 8 of 19 entry jobs pay less than basic living in a full work week today
  (Chop Bar, Market Trading, Radio & TV, Nightlife, Boxing, Fishing, Fashion, Ministry). After the proposal: 3 (Radio & TV, Nightlife, Ministry).
  Short-week careers (e.g. Ministry, three days) stay below by design: they suit part-time play.
- Time compression does not change daily income: the once-per-day cap is unchanged, so the
  active-work rate per real day is identical; only the real waiting falls from 60 to ~3–4 minutes.
- Auto-work pays 70% minus the commute, progresses at half speed and catches up at most
  3 offline days, so idle accounts cannot accumulate unbounded income.
- Entry training is free for most careers. The paid routes (Teller GH₵60, Coding GH₵120,
  Teaching GH₵80, Media GH₵50, Hospital admin GH₵250, Aviation GH₵300,
  Leadership GH₵400) are one-off sinks that a Hustler can reach within one to three weeks of entry work.
