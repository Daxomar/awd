# Stage 5 — employment and career system 2.0

Local implementation and authenticated browser acceptance pass. Exact-commit CI
pending. The salary rebalance is a proposal awaiting approval; no salary changed.

## Features completed

- **Time compression (documented).** One fictional 8-hour shift = one active work
  session of the career's three workplace assignments, back to back (40/55/70 s plus
  walking; measured 3.8 real minutes in the browser) = one paid shift per real calendar
  day, the same cap as before. A completed session applies the whole shift's
  performance, skill and needs effects; the legacy one-hour attendance path remains
  as a fallback for shifts already in progress. Restarting cannot claim a second wage.
- **Qualifications for all 19 careers** (`server/src/game/careers.js` `CAREER_REQS`):
  entry and promotion certificates per rank on top of the existing skill ladder. Free
  entry routes remain (8 free courses; 9 careers need no course to start). Examples:
  Bank Teller needs Basic Financial Literacy, Customer Service and Teller Training;
  Tech Intern needs Technical Fundamentals and Junior Developer the Practical Coding
  Simulation; senior roles (rank 4–5) need Leadership & Management, which itself
  needs rank-3 experience, so a new player cannot become CTO. Existing employees keep
  their jobs and ranks.
- **15 in-game courses**: learning objective, fee (free to GH₵400, paid once at
  enrolment), prerequisites, three original fictional scenario questions, server-side
  grading (answers never reach the client), pass mark, 10-minute retry cooldown,
  persistent certificate (`state.certs`), XP reward. Taken in person at an existing
  location (University, Tech Hub, bank, chop bar, Correct Cars, police, AMA, hospitals,
  radio station, airport) through a new *Courses here* option.
- **Career guidance.** Jobs shows every career's status; a locked career lists why
  (missing certificates with fee and location, missing skills and how to train them),
  the courses to take (tap to open, travel route if elsewhere) and the full ladder with
  each rank's requirements and pay.
- **Auto-work** (Jobs → Auto-work): enable/disable, transport (any non-car mode) and a
  daily round-trip fare limit (≤ GH₵500). Server-validated bounded settlement after the
  work window closes: employment, qualifications, work days, custody, serious illness,
  home to commute from, fare limit and affordability; 70% of the shift wage, half
  promotion credit, no stars; at most 3 offline days caught up; never on a day the
  player worked themself; never interrupts an activity; results listed in Jobs and
  journaled.
- **Salary audit and proposal** (`docs/master/stage5/salary-proposal.md`, generated by
  `server/loadtest/salary-proposal.mjs`): before/after tables for all 19 careers,
  normalised per shift and per week against rent, food and transport.

## Regressions found and fixed

- `walkTo` with an empty path never called its arrival callback, so tapping a
  station, seat or door the player was already standing on did nothing (affected
  every player, not only tests). Fixed.
- Auto-work treated an active shift today as covering all earlier days. Fixed with a
  regression assertion.
- "Shifts are 1 real hour" texts in the hired card, shift toast and Jobs app updated.

## Known limitations

- Day careers run 06:00–22:00 UTC (existing rule); the browser session used the
  Nightlife career because the run was at night. The unit test covers the Tech session.
- Auto-work's commute uses home-to-workplace distance; it does not render a ride.
