# Stage 12.2 — economy balance report and proposal

Generated by `server/loadtest/stage12-economy.mjs` (raw numbers: `economy-simulation.json`). Nothing in the game
was changed. Every recommendation below needs the owner's approval before it is activated.

## Method

A seeded Monte Carlo cadence model: 200 players per scenario, 90 in-game days, both natural origins (Hustler
GH₵800, Dbee GH₵25,000) and six play styles. It uses the live catalogue: career pay per shift and work days,
course fees, first eligible home and weekly rent, tax bands, trotro fares (GH₵24 return for 6 km), meals and data
bundles, the capped offline meals on return, auto-work rules (70% pay, 3-day catch-up), the real bounded
business-demand function for a provision stall, and the life-event limits (≤3 a day, losses ≤GH₵200 each and
≤GH₵250 a day).

Assumptions (stated so they can be challenged): new players take the best-paid entry job whose first courses are
free (Tech, GH₵150 a shift, 5 days a week); no promotions inside 90 days (the rules engine run in
`loadtest/econ_sim.js` shows a casual Trotro Mate still unpromoted after 21 days); 10% of planned online days are
missed; life events lose money 30% of the time and pay a small amount 40% of the time; no top-ups, First Friend
cash, gifts or trading between players. It is a model of the rules, not a forecast of real player behaviour.

"Basic stability" means two weeks of essentials (rent, three meals a day, work fares, data) in hand and a
non-negative trailing week.

**Time to qualify for careers.** Qualifying is quick: every entry course is a short in-game course with an
assessment, so a player qualifies on day 0–1. Fees at entry: 13 of 19 careers cost nothing; Bank GH₵60, Teacher
GH₵80, Doctor GH₵250, Aviation GH₵300. Promotions take 3, 5, 8 and 12 shifts at each rank plus skill and, for
senior ranks, the GH₵400 leadership course.

**Time to start a business.** A provision stall needs GH₵450 plus stock. Both origins can open one on day 0, but
a Hustler who opens a stall without a job loses it: the stall makes about GH₵5 a week once the wallet can no
longer restock it. With a job, the same stall adds about GH₵151 a week.

**Time to buy an entry property.** The cheapest unit costs GH₵5,000. At the median net rate a working Hustler
needs about 58 weeks (employment) or 24 weeks (job plus stall). A Dbee can buy on day 0. Apartments start at
GH₵1.6 million and a Vitz costs GH₵95,000; neither is reachable for a Hustler in the first year.

**Money created and destroyed per player over 90 days (average).** Created: salaries 3,619, stall withdrawals
1,629, life-event gains 958. Destroyed: rent 9,984, food 2,644, stall stock 1,302, life-event losses 1,134,
transport 592, data 336, tax 0. Rent is the dominant sink, mostly from Dbee homes. The economy is net
deflationary for these styles, so the inflation risk from gameplay income is low. The real inflation risks are
outside this model: player transfers, which only move money but concentrate it, and any future reward that
creates money (the First Friend cash rewards stay disabled).

**Wealth distribution.** Across all simulated players after 90 days the Gini coefficient is 0.57 and the top 20%
hold 53% of the money. Almost all of the gap comes from the starting origin, not from play.

## Findings

1. **Dbee players lose about GH₵1,200–1,800 a week.** Osu rent is GH₵1,600 a week, above any entry salary. A
   Dbee who does not move or reach a senior job is down to a few thousand cedis within 3–4 months. That can be
   intended (a fall from comfort), but it needs to be a visible goal, not a silent drain.
2. **A Hustler cannot live without a job.** Business-only, social and casual Hustlers miss 3–11 weeks of rent in
   90 days. The capped offline meals (3 × GH₵22 on every return) and weekly rent outrun occasional play.
3. **Auto-work alone does not support a player** who comes online once a week, because catch-up is limited to
   3 days. That matches the design intent (auto-work supplements play), but the phone should say so plainly.
4. **The provision stall is a supplement, not a living**, which is consistent with the Stage 8 business model.
5. **No tax is collected** at entry incomes (the first GH₵1,000 a week is untaxed), so tax is not a sink until
   senior ranks.
