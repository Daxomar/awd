# Stage F: Your First Friend in Accra

Phone → Meet is optional. A player in their first seven days can write a respectful hello. The server picks a real connected, onboarded, eligible player **on the current server**, checks privacy/block preferences and notifies the recipient. No NPC/offline/fake fallback. If none is available, wait, retry later or explore public hubs. Cross-server discovery is not added; existing cross-server private messages still work.

Pending requests expire after two minutes. Acceptance creates the existing `dm_links` consent and `dms` introduction atomically. No message is delivered before consent. A declined/expired/cancelled pair is not offered again; declines also give the recipient a ten-minute quiet period. Opt-out cancels outstanding introductions. At most one outgoing and one incoming active request per account, five requests/hour and a 30-second rematch cooldown. Status and notifications survive reconnect through PostgreSQL. Accepted conversations have 24 hours for completion.

Completion requires a subsequent recipient-to-initiator **stored text DM**, at least 12 letters/digits after normalization, different from the introduction. Client flags, short acknowledgments, money requests and fabricated DM IDs cannot complete it. This is a reasonable eligibility rule, not proof that a conversation is sincere. Failed post-message processing is reconciled against stored replies once a minute. Blocking and private-message preferences remain enforced.

`intro_completions` gives each initiating account exactly one completion. Reward rows and credits commit with completion and are unique per match/account. First-friend cash is **disabled by default**. Enabling `FIRST_FRIEND_REWARDS_ENABLED=1` uses configurable `FIRST_FRIEND_NEW_REWARD` (0–1000, proposed default 1000) and `FIRST_FRIEND_HOST_REWARD` (0–500, proposed default 500). Snapshot amounts are saved per match; disabling rewards prevents future fulfillment even for an existing match. No retroactive payment for already completed zero-cash introductions.

Before production activation, review the economy impact in `engagement-audit.md`. Paid host rewards require account age ≥24 hours, at most one rewarded welcome per rolling day, maximum three lifetime. Self-matches, previous DM pairs, repeat matched pairs, opted-out and blocked players are excluded. Moderation and existing transfer-age/daily limits remain. Multiple fresh accounts controlled by one person remain a risk: no invasive fingerprinting/phone collection is added and this system is **not abuse-proof**. Observe aggregate completion/reward anomalies and review before enabling cash.

Additive tables: `intro_profiles`, `intro_matches`, `intro_completions`, `intro_rewards`. No historical account/birth/balance mutation. Keep reward/credit history when rolling back the feature. There is no public deployment or reward activation in this milestone.
