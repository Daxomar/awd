# Stage C: approved advertising inventory

The original 12 placement IDs, prices and existing campaigns are retained. Additional proposed stock uses existing weekly bands: Madina and Tema commercial forecourt billboards GH₵800; Accra Mall ground panel GH₵800; Oxford commercial courtyard panel GH₵1,200; Airport hotel event forecourt panel GH₵1,000. One to twelve weeks, real GHS; no land transfer or game-money change. These are simulated approved surfaces, not claims to rent real-world property. No production stock is published by this local implementation.

`shared/ad-placements.js` (re-exported as `ads.js:SLOTS`) defines geographic coordinates, dimensions, orientation, surface class and display offset. `ad_placements` persists this catalogue. Ground stock is restricted to three designated paved commercial/event panels, excluding beaches, parks, landmarks, residential plots and road corridors. The strategic map uses symbolic readable dimensions (rather than geography-scale centimetre-thin billboards); ground faces are horizontal, offset above their frame with polygon offset to prevent z-fighting. Ground footprints and borders are checked against the sourced map constraints and compiled buildings, with 420 m symbolic separation from billboard anchors. The location preview temporarily moves closer and restores the regular map limits when closed. No local street collision or entrance coordinates are changed.

New campaigns hold a placement seat for 30 minutes. A unique partial index prevents two live reservations occupying a seat, and placement row locks serialize capacity checks with legacy campaigns. Payment verification moves the reservation to awaiting moderation; approval schedules it, expiration releases it. Cancellation releases unpaid bookings. Late verified payments do not take another advertiser's seat: they enter refund review. Old queued exclusive orders retain scheduling behavior. No automatic refund is promised; refunds require an operator/provider process.

Signed-in advertisers use Phone → My Ads for catalogue, dimensions, duration/price, raster upload, map-location preview, checkout resumption, status, cancellation and performance. Artwork: PNG/JPEG/WebP, ≤4 MB, ≤10 megapixels; Sharp decodes/re-encodes to 1024×512 JPEG, strips active content and metadata. HTML/SVG/executable uploads are rejected. Private pending artwork is owner/admin-only. Already-active artwork remains viewable in game.

Client exposure sampling runs at most twice per second, with at most two candidate occlusion checks per sample. It requires a live campaign, loaded artwork, a visible front face entirely inside the camera, at least 400 CSS pixels of projected area, two of three clear rays without blocking UI and two seconds of continuous visibility. Preview, background tab and open phone/sheet do not count. A campaign/viewer/15-minute uniqueness key bounds impressions and clicks server-side, with authenticated session/rate checks and revalidation of active campaign. Counts remain **estimates**, vulnerable to a modified client. No impression is recorded just because an object exists.

PostgreSQL stores aggregate daily impressions/clicks and deduplicated account IDs on a 90-day retention cutoff with daily cleanup; reports expose only aggregate metrics and 90-day unique viewers. No personal phone/IP/card data in exposure records. Clicks mean opening a campaign's board/panel, not proof of a sponsor-site conversion. Historic totals remain after viewer retention pruning. No guaranteed impressions or fraud-proof claims.

New additive tables: `ad_placements`, `ad_reservations`, `ad_exposures`, `ad_metrics_daily`. Roll back application code while retaining campaign/payment ledgers and original `ad_orders`; do not drop active reservations or paid records.
